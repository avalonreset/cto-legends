## Actual research cost

Provider, finite queries, locale, collection date, interpretation, exclusions
and reported cost. Do not equate expenditure with quality or claim a ranking
change without measurements.

## Artifact identity

List artifact name, SHA-256, source commit and download-back verification.
The catalog archive checksum is distinct from wheel and source-distribution
checksums. Record each exact artifact rather than reusing a similar filename.

## Final public state

Release URL, CI URL, catalog version/commit, repository URL, profile URL,
verified social-image identity and remaining limitations. Separate observed
results from recommendations or work still pending.
